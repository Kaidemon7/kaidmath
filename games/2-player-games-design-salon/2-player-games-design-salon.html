The Three Design Categories

Rounds rotate through three distinct challenges, each with its own toolset:

  • Nail Art — polish a full set, layer patterns and add gems and stickers to each nail.
  • Phone Case — choose a base shell, then stack decals, charms and finishes into a cohesive look.
  • Dessert Decoration — frost, top and garnish a treat so it reads as sweet and stylish.

Controls & Input Guide

Controls stay deliberately simple for this click and drag design game: click to select the different materials and colors available, then drag elements onto your creation to shape your proposal. On touchscreens the same tap-and-drag logic applies, which is why it works as a play 2 player games design salon no download option for phones and Chromebooks alike.

Advanced Tips to Win the Design Battle

Because aesthetics decide rounds, strategy beats speed:

  • Lock a color theme first, then add accents — matching tones read as more intentional than random clutter.
  • Leave negative space; over-decorating a nail or case often lowers the "designed" feel.
  • Scout the category before touching the palette so you spend your seconds placing, not browsing.
  • In Dessert Decoration, balance the garnish across the whole treat instead of piling one corner.
