What You Actually Do in Gacha life 1

In Gacha life 1 you build anime characters from scratch, dress them across 20 customizable slots, then stage them in Studio Mode scenes, chat with NPCs in Life Mode, and spin the Gacha to win gifts. This is the original 2018 title from Lunime — fans call it "Gacha life 1" to separate it from Gacha Life 2 (2023). If you came here to play Gacha life 1 online, know that the first 50 seconds are all about the wardrobe: pick a hair style, eye color, outfit, and pose before you ever touch a mini game.

Lunime's Luni first teased the game in a March 2018 Discord chat as "a new improved version of Gacha Studio" with more body parts and colors. It debuted on mobile in October 2018, with the desktop version arriving in early November.

The Three Modes That Make Gacha life 1 Tick

Studio Mode: 20 Slots of Customization

Studio Mode gives you 20 customizable slots to place characters, set backgrounds, add speech bubbles, and compose skits. This is where the "gacha life characters" and "gacha life customization" communities live — swap hairstyles, tweak eye colors, layer outfits, and pose for edits.

Life Mode: NPC Friendships

Life Mode drops you into towns full of NPCs to chat with. Every conversation raises a Friendship Status you can track, which also flags NPCs you haven't unlocked yet. Progressing through Life Mode is how you level up and open new features.

Gacha Games: 8 Mini Games for Gems

Learning gacha life how to play means mastering the gacha life mini games. There are 8 mini games, each themed around in-game characters. They aren't endless — you get a set number of lives, and some award bonus gems based on your score. Those gems fund the Gacha itself.

How to Earn Gems in Gacha life 1

Gems are the currency for spinning Gacha gifts. Grind the 8 mini games (chase the per-game bonus gem payouts) and progress Life Mode to level up. Spend gems on Gacha pulls to collect gifts, then use unlocks to expand your Studio Mode roster.

Advanced Tips for Higher Gem Runs

  • Prioritize mini games that offer the score-based bonus — a strong run pays more gems than a short one.
  • Don't burn all your lives early; the game ends when lives run out, so play conservatively for a longer streak.
  • Max out easy NPC friendships first in Life Mode to hit level-up thresholds faster.

Gacha life 1 vs 2: Which Are You Playing?

The gacha life 1 vs 2 question is the top reason people search the "1" suffix. Gacha life 1 is the original 2018 release. Gacha Life 2 launched in October 2023 with new systems. Gacha Club (2020) is a separate title that mixes dress-up with more role-play. If you want the classic, you want Gacha life 1.

For Parents: Content Notes

A 2020 update removed suggestive elements — but only on Android and PC versions. In August 2023, Luni updated the Android build with assets from Gacha Club. The game is a creative, free dress-up sim aimed at younger creators.

Frequently asked questions

How many mini games are in Gacha life 1 and how do they give gems?+
There are 8 mini games, each themed around in-game characters. You get a set number of lives per game rather than endless play, and some mini games hand out bonus gems based on how well you score. Those gems then fund your Gacha gift pulls.
What's the difference between Gacha life 1 and Gacha Life 2?+
Gacha life 1 is the original title Lunime released in 2018. Gacha Life 2 arrived in October 2023 with reworked systems. "Gacha life 1" is a community nickname to distinguish the classic from the sequel — it's not an official Lunime name.
How do you unlock new NPCs in Life Mode?+
Chat with NPCs to raise your Friendship Status, which the game tracks per character. The Friendship screen also shows which NPCs you haven't unlocked yet. Progressing through Life Mode conversations levels you up and opens new features.
How many customization slots does Studio Mode have in Gacha life 1?+
Studio Mode gives you 20 customizable slots. You can place characters, choose backgrounds, add speech bubbles, and pose everyone to build unique scenes and skits — the core of the game's creative community.
Was Gacha life 1 changed to be safer for kids?+
Yes. A 2020 update removed suggestive elements, though that change only reached the Android and PC versions. In August 2023, the Android build was also updated with assets from Gacha Club.
