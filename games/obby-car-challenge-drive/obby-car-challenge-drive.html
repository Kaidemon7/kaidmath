Obby Car Challenge: Drive is a browser driving game in which you choose a car, navigate through an obstacle course and race to the finish line. It runs in HTML5 on desktop and mobile browsers, so there is nothing to download. Each course is built around obstacles that test your steering and timing.

What you do

Your job in every run is to get your car to the finish line while avoiding the obstacles on the course. Obstacle courses like this reward steady driving more than reckless speed, because a crash costs you time. The game is built around the idea of driving farther and learning each track as you go.

Driving a course also feeds the wider progression. Players report that distance driven earns energy, which can be used to strengthen the car and unlock new content. The official listing describes collecting pets that boost speed, energy and rewards, along with upgrading cars and exploring maps with ramps, loops, jumps and traps.

How to Play Obby Car Challenge: Drive

  1. Pick a car from the selection before you start, then launch the course.
  2. Use the steering keys to move through the first stretch and learn how your car responds to acceleration and turns.
  3. Watch the obstacles ahead and adjust your line before you reach them, rather than correcting after a collision.
  4. Keep moving toward the finish line. Reaching it is the goal of each course, and a faster finish is the main measure of your run.
  5. After a run, use what you earned to improve your car and try the course again with a cleaner line.

What changes between sessions

Courses vary in layout. Players describe tracks with rolling hills, floating platforms, shifting slopes and tight corners, so the same car handles each section differently. Your car can be upgraded over time, and pets can be collected to boost speed, energy and rewards, according to the official description. Because each course asks for different timing, progress comes from learning the layouts rather than from one fixed route.

Tips and strategy

  • Slow down on your first run and memorise where the obstacles sit. Familiar patterns make later runs faster.
  • Take a smoother path when one is available. A clean line usually beats a risky shortcut that ends in a crash.
  • Time your jumps and turns before you commit, especially on floating platforms and slopes.
  • Use the camera control to look ahead when you need to plan the next section.
  • Upgrade your car as energy comes in, and favour the upgrades that help the handling you struggle with most.
  • If you are on a phone, practise steering with the virtual joystick on a short section before attempting a full course.
