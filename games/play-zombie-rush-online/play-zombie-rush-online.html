How to Play

Survive as long as you can by steering your survivor away from the advancing zombies. Move by holding and dragging with the mouse, using WASD or the arrow keys, or dragging your finger on a touch screen.
  1. 1. Start your survivor

    Open the game and wait for your survivor to appear on screen before you begin moving.

  2. 2. Direct with the mouse or keys

    On a computer, hold left click and drag, or use WASD or the arrow keys to steer your character.

  3. 3. Lead with touch

    On a phone or tablet, tap anywhere and drag your finger so your survivor follows it.

  4. 4. Keep moving through the horde

    Change direction early so the zombies cannot pin your survivor against an edge.

Zombie Rush is a top-down survival shooter you play in the browser on ZGames.io. You control a lone survivor who has to keep moving as the zombies close in. Because the game is built with HTML5, you can start a round straight from the page without installing anything.

What You Do in Zombie Rush

Your job is to keep your survivor alive while the zombies press in from around you. The core loop is simple: steer your character through the crowd, stay out of the way of the undead, and keep going for as long as you can. Pressure builds as a run goes on, so the longer you survive, the harder it becomes to find open ground.

Movement is the central skill. Your survivor follows your pointer or your keyboard input, so every decision about where to go is also a decision about where the zombies will follow.

How to Play Zombie Rush

  1. Open the game and wait for the survivor to appear on screen.
  2. On a computer, hold the left mouse button and drag to direct your character, or use WASD or the arrow keys to move.
  3. On a phone or tablet, tap anywhere and drag your finger to lead your survivor.
  4. Keep moving so the zombies do not reach you, and watch how the crowd shifts as you change direction.
  5. Keep the run going for as long as you can survive.

On desktop, your character follows the direction of your mouse from its current position. On touch screens, the survivor follows your finger as you drag. The game supports both portrait and landscape modes and adjusts to your screen automatically.

What Changes Between Sessions

No separate modes, levels, characters or upgrades are documented for Zombie Rush, so this guide does not describe any. Each session is a single-player run where the challenge comes from the zombies gathering around you.

Tips and Strategy for Zombie Rush

  • Keep your survivor moving rather than standing still, since a stationary character gives the crowd time to close in.
  • Drag in wide arcs instead of sharp zigzags so you keep distance from the nearest zombies.
  • Look ahead to open ground before the crowd forms a wall in front of you.
  • Change direction early so the zombies cannot pin your survivor against an edge.
  • On a phone, use short, smooth drags so your finger does not overshoot the direction you want.
  • On a computer, pick either the mouse or WASD and stick with it so your input stays consistent under pressure.
  • If you play on a tablet or phone, try both portrait and landscape to see which gives you a clearer view of the horde.
