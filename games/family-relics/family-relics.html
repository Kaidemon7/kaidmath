In Family Relics online you inherit Uncle Archie's run-down farm in the town of Willow Hills, and the very first thing you do is gather timber to patch a leaky roof before manager Donald unlocks the trading post. From there the loop is pure farm-sim: plant corn, raise chickens, bake bread, and export goods by boat while other players trade alongside you in the same 3D world.

This is the syndicated Family Nest Family Relics title from developer Social Infinite, a business-simulation follow-up to Farm Days. It runs entirely in HTML5, so play Family Relics free with no download on desktop, mobile, or tablet — an account is optional and only needed to save progress across devices.

What separates it from single-screen clickers is the social layer: this is a light MMO where you can trade surplus eggs and flour, join community events, and expand across water once the boat is repaired.

The Uncle Archie Farm Loop in Family Relics online

Your objective is to rebuild the estate task by task. You gather resources, construct buildings, plant crops, and raise animals, and every repaired structure feeds the next: repair the mill to make flour, then bake bread, then sell finished goods for coins. As you push outward you unlock new areas, discover hidden treasures, and uncover your family's history.

Energy, Coins and Diamonds

Almost every action costs energy. When you run dry, do NOT wait — how to get more energy in Family Relics comes down to the map: search for hedgehogs and click them, and each gives 1–2 energy back. Coins come from selling produce; diamonds are the premium currency for speeding builds.

Family Relics controls

The Family Relics controls are mouse-only, which makes it ideal for Chromebooks and touchscreens. Use the mouse (or a tap) to navigate; the left mouse button interacts with everything — planting, harvesting, chatting to Donald, and clicking hedgehogs for energy.

  • Left click — interact, plant, harvest, collect
  • Mouse drag — pan the camera around the farm
  • Tap (mobile/tablet) — same as left click

Family Relics tips and tricks for a fast start

  1. Prioritise chickens early — buy chicks, grow them into hens, and stack egg output for reliable income.
  2. Unblock and repair the mill and bakery first so you can turn wheat into flour and flour into bread.
  3. Repair the boat as soon as you can afford it — it opens the far lands and new trade opportunities.
  4. Do a hedgehog sweep before big harvests so a low energy bar never stalls a session.

Is Family Relics multiplayer?

Yes — the Family Relics farm game is a light 3D MMO. You can trade with other players, join community events, and cooperate to clear obstacles, so the world feels alive rather than static.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get more energy in Family Relics?+
Search the map for hedgehogs and click on them — each one gives you 1 to 2 energy. Do a quick sweep before large harvests so you never stall mid-task waiting for energy to recharge.
What are the controls for Family Relics?+
It's entirely mouse-driven. Use the mouse to navigate and the left mouse button to interact with everything — planting, harvesting, talking to Donald, and clicking hedgehogs. On mobile and tablet, tapping does the same job.
Is Family Relics the same as Family Nest?+
Yes. It's syndicated across portals under both 'Family Relics' and 'Family Nest: Family Relics.' Same Uncle Archie farm, same Willow Hills setting, same developer Social Infinite — just different titles on different sites.
Is Family Relics multiplayer?+
It is a light 3D MMO farming game. You run your own farm but can trade goods with other players, join community events, and team up to clear obstacles, so you share the world with other gamers.
What should I build first in Family Relics?+
Fix the farmhouse roof for Donald, then focus on chickens for steady egg income. Next repair the mill and bakery to make flour and bread, and save up to unblock the boat, which opens new lands to explore.
