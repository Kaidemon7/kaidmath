What You Actually Do in Undead Invasion Online

In Undead Invasion online you defend a single crumbling building — a dilapidated orphanage that is your last remaining shelter — against constant waves of undead that get more intense every in-game day. You move left and right along the ground floor, aim your weapon at approaching zombies, kick or melee anything that closes distance, and toss grenades into tight clusters. Between waves you drop barricades and traps to buy yourself time. It is a side-scrolling zombie survival game with a brutal catch: there are no checkpoints.

What makes it a true roguelike zombie shooter is permadeath. The moment your character dies, you start from day one with nothing. Every coin you earned, every weapon you bought, every trap layout you perfected — gone. That is why smart players treat each run as a fresh strategy puzzle rather than a mindless zombie defense game.

The Undead Invasion Day-and-Wave Survival Loop

Each day is built from escalating attack waves. You earn coins by killing zombies and by surviving the full day, then spend that money on stronger firearms, melee upgrades, health items, and better defenses. The loop is: survive the wave, cash out, reinforce, repeat. Progression is entirely gear-driven — the further you push, the tougher and more numerous the undead become, so falling behind on upgrades is what usually ends a run.

Weapons, Traps, and Barricades

You carry firearms and melee options, with grenades reserved for large clusters that a single bullet can't handle. Before or between waves you place barricades to funnel zombies and drop traps where the horde bunches up. Placement matters far more than quantity — a trap in the wrong spot wastes coins you'll desperately need next day.

Undead Invasion Controls and How to Play

Undead Invasion uses keyboard and mouse, with the exact bindings shown on the in-game screen when you start. Practically, you move with directional keys, aim and fire toward the incoming side, melee or kick when a zombie gets too close, and use a key to throw grenades or lay defenses. Keeping distance is the golden rule: zombies only deal damage when they reach you, so backpedaling while firing is your default survival stance.

Undead Invasion Tips and Strategy for Longer Runs

  • Manage distance first, damage second — retreat as you shoot so no zombie ever lands a hit; a full-health run outlasts a high-damage one.
  • Save grenades for clusters — never waste one on a single walker; wait until three or more line up.
  • Buy defenses before luxury weapons early — traps and barricades multiply the value of a weak starting gun.
  • Prioritize survival bonuses — finishing the full day pays extra coins, so don't gamble a guaranteed payout on a risky push.
