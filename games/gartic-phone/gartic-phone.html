What Is Gartic Phone?

Gartic Phone is a unique online game that combines drawing and guessing in a series of entertaining rounds. Players take turns creating images based on a prompt while others try to guess what it is. This multiplayer game thrives on creativity, humor, and often unexpected outcomes that lead to laughter, making it perfect for parties or casual get-togethers. The game is easy to understand, making it suitable for players of all ages.

Gartic Phone Gameplay Overview

The gameplay of Gartic Phone involves a group of players who each get a chance to draw or guess prompts. Initially, a prompt is provided, and one player draws it on the virtual canvas. The next player then sees the drawing and must guess what it depicts. The twist is that between rounds, players can only see the previous interpretation – leading to hilarious interpretations as the original prompt transforms through each turn. Grafics are colorful and whimsical, making the game visually appealing while ensuring an enjoyable experience overall.
